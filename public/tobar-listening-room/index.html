Unofficial Extension

A quiet, private research companion for Tobar an Dualchais

Tobar — Listening Room is an independent Chromium browser extension created by Ioannis Valasakis. It helps researchers, students, and enthusiasts curate private collections of recordings, preserve search histories, and capture structured field notes from Scotland's oral heritage archive.

✉ Email wizofe.dev@gmail.com Read Privacy Policy Visit Tobar an Dualchais ↗

Key capabilities

Built from scratch to support deep listening and archival discovery without distraction.

🎙

Archival Capture

Click the extension while browsing any recording or search on Tobar an Dualchais to extract track IDs, titles, contributors, and full catalogue metadata for review.

📁

Curated Collections

Group discoveries into named research folders. Add personal notes, tags, favourite flags, and manual listening marks while keeping archive data intact.

🔍

Search & Snapshots

Save search queries with exact URLs and filter sets, or freeze dated snapshots of result pages so you never lose track of recordings as catalogues evolve.

🔒

100% Browser-Local

No accounts, zero telemetry, no tracking, and no external servers. Everything is stored securely in your browser's local IndexedDB database.

📦

Obsidian & Backups

One-click complete JSON export and restore for safety, plus clean Markdown ZIP exports tailored for Obsidian or personal knowledge bases.

⚖️

Respect for the Archive

Audio playback happens directly on the official Tobar an Dualchais website. Listening Room respects copyright and never redistributes audio files.

How it works

1

Browse the Archive

Explore recordings, stories, songs, and field collections on the official Tobar an Dualchais website.

2

Capture with One Click

Click the extension toolbar icon. Review the extracted metadata and catalogue tags, then save the recording, search query, or result snapshot.

3

Annotate & Export

Open your collection in a dedicated tab to write research notes, organise tags, or export complete backups and Markdown summaries.
